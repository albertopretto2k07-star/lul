FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I upgrade or downgrade at any time?
Yes. Change your plan anytime from your dealer dashboard. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades at the end of the billing period.
Is there a long-term contract?
No. All plans are month-to-month. Cancel anytime with no penalty. Annual billing saves 17% but is also cancellable.
How does the referral bonus work?
Each dealer gets a unique referral link. When a new dealer activates a paid plan using your link, you receive listing credits equivalent to one month of their plan. Credits never expire.
What is the revenue share model?
Available on Enterprise, revenue share means you pay a 5–10% commission on successfully facilitated sales, with no monthly subscription fee. Best for high-value, low-volume inventory.
How does the API inventory feed work?
On Elite and Enterprise, connect your inventory management system via our RESTful API or XML/CSV feed. Listings sync automatically. Full docs and onboarding call included.
What payment methods do you accept?
All major credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), SEPA bank transfer, and PayPal. Annual invoices available for Enterprise clients.
