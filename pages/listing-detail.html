Description

This exceptional 2024 Ferrari SF90 Stradale with the coveted Assetto Fiorano package represents the pinnacle of Ferrari's engineering prowess. Finished in Rosso Corsa with a Nero interior, this hybrid supercar combines a twin-turbocharged V8 with three electric motors to produce a staggering 986 horsepower.

The Assetto Fiorano package adds Multimatic shock absorbers, high downforce carbon fibre rear spoiler, front splitter, and underbody, titanium exhaust with Inconel pipes, and carbon fibre door panels — shedding 30 kg from the standard SF90.

Delivered new through an authorised Ferrari dealer in Monaco, this example has covered just 1,200 km from new. Full Ferrari service history with the next service not due until 2026. The car comes with its complete accessory package including indoor car cover, battery charger, and tool kit.
